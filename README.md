
# CampusMatch – AI-Powered Campus Lost & Found

An AI-powered Lost and Found management platform built on **ServiceNow** to help students report lost and found items and identify potential matches through intelligent image analysis and automated workflows.

## Problem Statement

Managing lost and found items on college campuses is often difficult due to scattered reports, manual searching, and the inability to identify matching items efficiently.

CampusMatch simplifies this process by bringing lost and found reports into one platform and using AI capabilities to identify potential matches.

## Key Features

- **Lost & Found Reporting:** Submit lost and found item reports with relevant details and images.
- **AI-Based Image Extraction:** Uses ServiceNow Document Intelligence to extract relevant attributes from uploaded images.
- **Intelligent Similarity Matching:** Uses Predictive Intelligence to identify potential matches between lost and found reports.
- **AI-Powered Title Generation:** Uses Now Assist to generate privacy-preserving titles for item listings.
- **Weighted Match Scoring:** Evaluates matching attributes to identify relevant potential matches.
- **Automated Workflows:** Uses Business Rules and workflow automation to support verification and claim processing.
- **Unclaimed Item Lifecycle:** Uses scheduled jobs to track unclaimed items, monitor their status, and trigger lifecycle updates based on predefined time periods.

## How It Works

1. A student submits a lost or found item report.
2. Relevant item details and images are collected.
3. Document Intelligence extracts attributes from uploaded images.
4. Predictive Intelligence helps identify potential matching reports.
5. Weighted match scoring helps prioritize relevant matches.
6. Students can proceed with verification and the item claim process through automated workflows.
7. Items that remain unclaimed are monitored through scheduled jobs, which manage their lifecycle based on predefined timelines.
